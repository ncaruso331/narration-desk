Narration Desk Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Narration Desk is made by Nadia Caruso. This policy describes how the iPhone and iPad app handles information.

We do not collect your data

The app does not require an account and does not include third-party analytics or advertising. We do not collect, sell, or share your recordings, transcripts, scripts, or photos. What you record stays on your device unless you choose to share a file or save a reel to Photos.

Microphone

The app uses the microphone to record voice takes and Visual Story voiceovers. Audio is written to files on your device.

Speech recognition

Transcripts use Apple speech APIs. Live recognition is requested on the device. After you mark a section or finish a take, iOS 26 or later may download Apple's speech model over the network. That model is provided by Apple, not bundled inside Narration Desk. If on-device recognition is not available, the app may ask Apple's speech service to transcribe a saved file, which can mean audio is sent to Apple. We do not receive that audio or the text Apple returns. It is stored on your device.

Photos

If you use Visual Story, you can pick a photo or video from your library and, if you want, save a finished reel to Photos. That happens only when you ask for it.

Settings on the device

Preferences such as theme and script drafts may be stored in app settings on your device so they are there the next time you open the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact
