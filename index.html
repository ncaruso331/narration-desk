CleanStitch

Support for the iPhone and iPad app. CleanStitch was called Narration Desk before version 1.0.4.

CleanStitch is a teleprompter and voice recorder for podcasts, audiobook narration, and voiceover. You can read a script while you record 48 kHz WAV takes, stitch takes into one file, clean up your voice, transcribe a take on your device, record audiobooks chapter by chapter, and add a voiceover to a photo or a short video.

Your recordings

Takes, transcripts, audiobook files, and reels stay on your device unless you share them or save a reel to Photos. There is no account. You can browse your files in the Files app, under On My iPhone or On My iPad, then CleanStitch.

Clean up

Clean up lowers room noise, rumble, and hum, removes mouth clicks, softens harsh S sounds, and evens out your volume. It saves a cleaned copy next to your take, so the original is always kept.

Audiobooks and ACX

An audiobook project has Opening Credits, chapters, Closing Credits, and a Retail Sample, each with its own script, takes, and status. Check ACX measures volume, peaks, background noise, room tone, and long pauses, and explains each result in plain English. Make ACX-ready fixes what it can on a copy and checks again. If a part still needs work, it tells you why and what to try.

Export makes a 192 kbps constant bit rate MP3 at 44.1 kHz for each section, and each MP3 is measured again after it is made. CleanStitch checks your files against the ACX audio requirements, but ACX reviews every audiobook itself before it goes on sale. CleanStitch is not made by or connected with Audible or ACX.

Phone calls while recording

If a call comes in while you record, your take pauses and everything so far is saved. After the call, tap Resume to carry on with the same take.

Transcripts

The app uses Apple speech features. On iOS 26 or later, your iPhone may download Apple's speech model the first time you transcribe. If on-device recognition is not available, Apple may transcribe a saved take using its own speech service. CleanStitch does not receive your audio or text.

Visual Story

While you talk, the original sound of a video is muted so your voice is clear. You do not need headphones. Pause and resume whenever you want. Let video play runs the clip at full original volume while you stay quiet.
