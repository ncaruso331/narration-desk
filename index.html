Narration Desk

Support for the iPhone and iPad app.

Narration Desk is a teleprompter and voice recorder. You can record 48 kHz WAV takes, read a script, transcribe a take on your device, stitch takes, and add a voiceover to a photo or a short video.

Your recordings

Takes, transcripts, and reels stay on your device unless you share them or save a reel to Photos. There is no account. On iPhone and iPad you can also browse files in the Files app, under On My iPhone or On My iPad, then Narration Desk.

Transcripts

The app uses Apple speech features. On iOS 26 or later, your iPhone may download Apple's speech model the first time you transcribe. If on-device recognition is not available, Apple may transcribe a saved take using its own speech service. Narration Desk does not receive your audio or text.

Visual Story

While you talk, the original sound of a video is muted so your voice is clear. You do not need headphones. Pause and resume whenever you want. Let video play runs the clip at full original volume while you stay quiet.
